Quick Answers

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Do you travel for shows? ↓

Yes — Lily is available to travel nationally and internationally for festivals, club events, and private performances. Travel logistics (flights, accommodation, rider) are factored into the booking rate. Home markets are Charlotte and Asheville, NC, where travel costs are minimal. Reach out early for touring season bookings — the calendar fills fast.

What do fire shows require? ↓

Fire performances need an approved outdoor or ventilated indoor space, a designated fire safety area with a spotter, and a flame-retardant performance surface (or confirmed clearance from your venue). A water/damp-towel safety setup must be on-site. Lily carries her own equipment and insurance documentation. Full tech rider and requirements will be shared after initial contact.

Wholesale apparel inquiries? ↓

Blue-Flame Designs is open to select wholesale partnerships with boutiques, festival merch vendors, and flow-arts retailers whose vibe aligns with the brand. Minimum order quantities, pricing tiers, and lead times vary by product line. Select "Wholesale / Apparel" from the inquiry type above and describe your shop or event — Lily will follow up with a wholesale deck.

Can you do custom apparel designs? ↓

Custom commission work is available on a selective basis — festival capsule collections, event-specific merch, and collaborative design projects. Capacity is limited and priority goes to projects with aligned aesthetics (dark, UV, rave, circus, fire). Include a brief description of your vision, timeline, and approximate quantity needs in your message and Lily will let you know if it's a fit.

01 — Brand Story

The Jackalope
of All Trades

"Heat you feel before
you hear it."

GHOST PEPPER is the creative alias of Liliya "Lily" Semenova — DJ, producer, fire performer, and apparel designer operating out of Charlotte and Asheville, North Carolina. The name says everything: the world's hottest fruit, the one that leaves a mark long after the moment has passed. Ghost Pepper is the burn that lingers.

Lily came up through the festival and rave circuit, where the DJ booth, the fire circle, and the merch table were never separate worlds — they were one continuous ritual. She plays drum & bass at the upper edge of the genre, around 174 BPM, where the groove gets tight enough to feel like controlled chaos. Off the decks, she spins poi, works fire fans, and designs apparel under the Blue-Flame Designs imprint — festival-ready pieces that hold up under blacklight and tell a story in the dark.

The "jackalope of all trades" phrase captures something real: a creature that shouldn't logically exist but absolutely does. Lily bridges disciplines that rarely overlap — the technical precision of DJ production, the embodied performance of fire arts, and the visual craft of apparel design. The brand is the connective tissue between all three.

Ghost Pepper doesn't chase mainstream. It sets the terms. It shows up for the people who want the heat, the bass, and the handmade piece they'll still be talking about at the next fest. The brand lives at the intersection of sound, fire, and threads — and it's built to burn.

Mission

To create visceral experiences — in sound, in spectacle, in what you wear home — that make the festival moment feel permanent.

Also Performs As

Blue-Flame Designs (@blueflame.designs) is the apparel-design arm — the commercial face of the creative practice. GHOST PEPPER is the full artist identity: the set, the fire, the fit, the feeling.

02 — Brand Personality

Who We
Are

Six words. Every creative decision — visual, verbal, sonic — should map back to at least one of them.

Fierce
Ghost Pepper doesn't ask permission. The brand asserts itself — in the mix, in the fire circle, in the design. There's no volume at which this is timid.
Hypnotic
The rhythm, the spinning flame, the blacklight pattern on a hoodie — each one is designed to hold attention. Ghost Pepper locks you in and doesn't let go until it's ready.
Untamed
Not chaotic — intentional. The genre is drum & bass, not noise. But the spirit is feral: untrained by convention, unimpressed by gatekeeping, unbothered by rules that don't serve the work.
Crafted
Every stitch, every mix, every fire line is deliberate. This is a polymath's brand, which means the bar for craft is high across all disciplines simultaneously. Sloppy doesn't fit here.
Communal
Raves, festivals, and fire circles are inherently collective. Ghost Pepper is born from that culture — the shared warmth of a crowd, the way bass unifies strangers. The brand is for the whole tribe.
Ritual
There's ceremony in the set, in the performance, in putting on the gear. Ghost Pepper treats each moment — the first drop, the first flame, the first wear — as something worth marking.

The Three Pillars

Sound

Drum & bass at ~174 BPM. DJ sets, live production, SoundCloud releases. The sonic identity is dense, precise, and relentless — bass-heavy sub frequencies anchored by breakbeats that don't let the crowd breathe until they want them to. Ghost Pepper's sound is the ritual itself; the other two pillars orbit it.

Fire

Poi, fire fans, and flow performance. Fire spinning at festivals is both art and community signal — it marks the people who go deep. Lily's fire practice is the brand's most visible live expression, the moment the Ghost Pepper mark becomes a living thing. Fire at night, at the edge of the stage, is the brand's most iconic image.

Threads

Blue-Flame Designs apparel. UV-reactive tees, hoodies, performance wear, and accessories designed for the festival environment — pieces that look one way under sunlight and transform under blacklight. The apparel line extends the brand into the crowd, making every wearer a walking GHOST PEPPER moment.

04 — Logo & Mark

The Mark

A ghost-pepper fruit rendered as a flame teardrop — pointing up, burning blue from within, watching with two black eyes. Neon-green stem. One symbol, three readings: pepper, ghost, flame.

Mark Anatomy

  • Outer body: Teardrop flame path — bottom hemisphere is the pepper body, the pointed tip is the flame apex. Filled with a vertical gradient: Pepper Red (#e11212) → Ember Orange (#ff5e1a) → warm gold (#ffd27a). Thin gold stroke reinforces the glow edge.
  • Inner blue flame core: A smaller teardrop centered inside the body, offset upward. Diagonal gradient: Deep Blue (#1e63ff) → Blue Flame (#00b3ff). Opacity 0.92 — slightly translucent to let the body gradient breathe behind it.
  • Ghost eyes: Two filled circles in Void Black (#05050a), symmetric at the pepper-hip level. Diameter ~2.6 units (in a 64-unit viewBox). The eyes read as ghost, skull, or anthropomorphic pepper depending on context.
  • Stem: Small triangular path at the apex, Neon Green / UV Green (#39ff14). The only element that breaks from the warm fire palette — deliberate UV-accent signal.
  • Wordmark: "GHOST PEPPER" in Bebas Neue, tracked at +6%. Kicker "GHOST.PEPPER.DNB" in Space Mono, tracked at +40%.

Clear Space & Sizing

  • Clear space: Minimum clear space on all sides equals the height of the stem element (approx. 1/8 of the mark height). Never crowd the mark with other text or graphic elements.
  • Minimum size — digital: 32px height for the mark-only; 140px width for the full wordmark lockup.
  • Minimum size — print: 12mm height for the mark-only; 50mm width for the full lockup.
  • Preferred backgrounds: Void Black, Char, or any dark surface. The brand lives in the dark.
Misuse — Don'ts
✗ Don't

Recolor the mark using off-palette colors, pastels, or plain black. The gradient is non-negotiable.

✗ Don't

Stretch, skew, or rotate the mark. The upward point is sacred — pepper tips up, always.

✗ Don't

Place the mark on a light background without the solid-fill on-light version. Never use the glowing version on white.

✗ Don't

Separate the kicker "GHOST.PEPPER.DNB" from the wordmark and use it as a standalone headline.

✗ Don't

Add drop shadows, bevels, or third-party effects. The glow is built in — don't add to it.

✗ Don't

Use the mark at sizes below minimum — the eye details and stem disappear and the mark reads as a blob.

UV & Blacklight Note

UV Green (#39ff14) and Plasma Purple (#b026ff) are specifically chosen to represent the brand's blacklight-reactive apparel. In digital contexts, these colors carry that UV-reactive energy through glow effects and neon contrast. In apparel production, these tones are specified using UV-reactive inks on suitable base fabrics. Blue Flame (#00b3ff) also pops strongly under blacklight — it's not accidental that the apparel line shares its name with the palette's coolest tone.

06 — Typography

Type System

Three typefaces. One for the stage, one for the page, one for the data. Each has a specific job and they don't trade places.

Display — Bebas Neue · All headlines, wordmark, section titles
96px GHOST PEPPER
64px FIRE · BASS · RITUAL
48px WEAR THE BURN
32px DRUM & BASS — 174 BPM
24px Charlotte / Asheville, North Carolina

Bebas Neue is a single-weight, all-caps condensed display face. Its extreme x-height and tight tracking make it read at massive sizes across posters, screens, and fabric prints. Never set Bebas Neue in mixed case. Never track it negatively — the default or slight positive track only. Pair always with Rajdhani or Space Mono for supporting text.

Body / UI — Rajdhani · Body copy, navigation, product descriptions, UI labels
700 · 24px Ghost Pepper DnB — Fire & Flow Performance
600 · 18px Liliya Semenova — DJ / Producer / Fire Artist / Apparel Designer
400 · 17px Ghost Pepper is the creative alias of Lily Semenova, a drum & bass DJ, fire performer, and festival apparel designer based in Charlotte and Asheville, NC.
500 · 14px UV-reactive ink — machine wash cold — festival-grade construction

Rajdhani is a semi-condensed humanist sans with strong optical weight at display sizes but excellent readability in body columns. Available weights 400–700. Use 400–500 for body, 600–700 for UI emphasis, sub-headings, and product names. Sentence case only in body and UI; all-caps permitted for very short labels (navigation, tags) at 600+ weight.

Mono / Labels — Space Mono · Captions, eyebrows, code, data labels, kicker text
700 · 14px GHOST.PEPPER.DNB · EST. CHARLOTTE NC
400 · 13px BPM: 174 · KEY: F# minor · RUNTIME: 58:32
eyebrow · 11px 07 — Color System
400 · 12px © 2026 Blue-Flame Designs / Ghost Pepper · @ghost.pepper.dnb

Space Mono is the brand's technical, data, and label voice. Its fixed-width, grid-locked letters connote precision and the producer mindset. Keep it small — 14px and below in most cases. Heavy tracking (0.2–0.45em) at smallest sizes. Color: Bone Dim for neutral labels; Ember Orange for active eyebrows and section markers. Never use Space Mono for body paragraphs.

Pairing Rules
  • Headline + Body: Bebas Neue headline → Rajdhani body copy. Never two display weights together.
  • Eyebrow + Title: Space Mono eyebrow (small, tracked wide) → Bebas Neue title. Classic hierarchy.
  • Product Labels: Rajdhani product name (600 weight) → Space Mono SKU/detail line below.
  • Never: Space Mono and Bebas Neue as equals at the same size — one must lead, one must follow.
07 — Imagery & Art Direction

The Visual World

Ghost Pepper imagery lives in the dark — fire-lit, UV-drenched, high-contrast. Every shot is either mid-performance or mid-ritual. No studio white, no lifestyle beige, no softbox flat light.

Mood Grid

Poi arcs at night, ember-to-void gradient sky
Blue Core — Macro Fire
Tight on the blue flame base, blurred orange corona
UV Body Paint — Blacklight
Neon green + plasma purple on skin, full dark surround

DJ silhouette, UV green edge, deep blue-black crowd
Festival Crowd — Blacklight

Fire Fan — High Speed
Frozen fire petals, warm gold bokeh, velvet black

Night / Dark Only

All hero imagery is set at night or in blacklight environments. Daytime lifestyle shots are not part of the Ghost Pepper visual vocabulary. If you shoot during the day, underexpose and grade toward night.

High Contrast

Maximize the contrast between the subject — fire, performer, glowing apparel — and the background void. Near-black shadows, blown-out flame highlights. No midtone comfort zones.

UV & Neon Accent

UV-reactive elements should visually dominate any blacklight shot. If UV green or plasma purple aren't present, add a rim light in those tones. The blacklight look is a core brand signal.

Performance First

The subject is always doing something — spinning, playing, moving. Ghost Pepper doesn't do posed portrait-mode lifestyle. Every image should show the act in progress or its immediate aftermath.

Psychedelic Grade

Color grading leans chromatic and slightly hallucinatory — pushed teals and oranges, deep lifted blacks in neon zones, slight bloom on fire sources. Reference: festival long-exposure photography meets dark editorial.

No Stock

Ghost Pepper is a real person doing real things. All imagery should be original — actual fire performances, actual DJ sets, actual apparel worn in the field. Stock photography reads false immediately in this community.

08 — Apparel Direction

Blue-Flame Designs

The apparel arm of Ghost Pepper. Festival-grade pieces designed to perform under blacklight, hold up in the pit, and still look right at the afterparty. Every item is an extension of the brand's visual identity — not merchandise, but wearable ritual objects.

👕
Tees
Heavyweight cotton or cotton-poly blends. Front/back graphic configurations. UV-reactive ink on select colorways. Black base standard; charcoal and bone as alternates.
🧥
Hoodies
Mid-weight fleece, festival-appropriate. Pullover and zip configurations. Kangaroo pocket, drop-shoulder cut. UV print on back panel or sleeve graphic.
⚡
Performance
Moisture-wicking stretch fabric for flow arts. Crop tops, leggings, rashguards. UV-reactive prints that survive sweat. Cut for movement — poling, spinning, dancing.
🧢
Headwear
5-panel and bucket hats. Embroidered Ghost Pepper mark or Blue-Flame logo. UV-reactive embroidery thread on select SKUs.
🎒
Accessories
Tote bags, bandanas, stickers, pins, patches. Festival-utility first. Stickers are the entry-level brand touchpoint — make them UV-reactive.
🖼️
Art Prints
Limited-edition giclée prints of key brand illustrations. Fire performance photography edits. UV-reactive ink options for collector prints.
UV-Reactive Note

UV-reactive (blacklight) ink is not an option on Ghost Pepper apparel — it's a design requirement on hero pieces. The apparel should transform when the lights go out. UV Green (#39ff14), Plasma Purple (#b026ff), and Blue Flame (#00b3ff) are the three UV priority colors. In production, work with print vendors who specialize in UV-reactive DTG or screen printing.
